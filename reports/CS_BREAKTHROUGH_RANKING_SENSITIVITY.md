## Stable and unstable conclusions

- **Stable candidate families:** repository-level proof/code synthesis, constrained LLM serving, adaptive privacy accounting, and the dynamic matching/ORS chain remain on the short list across the reasonable qualitative readings. This is selection robustness, not forecast validation.
- **Stable negative boundary:** no model probabilities, calibrated rates, or CS Paper 3 scores are supported under any weighting.
- **Unstable relative order:** DP versus inference, and matching versus specialized geometry, depend strongly on whether tractability or impact is prioritized.
- **Most fragile inclusion:** agents that independently discover new science. Benchmarks currently measure ideation or replication separately, and astronomy outcome taxonomy reliability is weak; this stays last in the medium-horizon list.
- **Correlated topics:** dynamic matching and ORS form one family; inference subtopics are grouped; code-proof and repository repair are grouped; the sensitivity table does not count them as independent confirmations.

No numeric weights or probabilities were used. Sensitivity was assessed by changing declared ordinal priorities and checking whether candidate membership and rank were stable. It cannot establish prospective calibration.
