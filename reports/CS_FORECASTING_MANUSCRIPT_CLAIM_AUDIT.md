# Manuscript claim-to-evidence audit

| Draft claim | Evidence actually available | Verdict / approved wording |
|---|---|---|
| “Evidence-grounded benchmark” | Ten qualitative frozen records and integrity tests; no mature labels, no external review, one date-conflicted event, selection-flow gaps | Do not say validated benchmark. Say “pilot event registry” and “protocol proposal”; benchmark quality is unestablished. |
| “Prospective forecast” | Event statements and directional qualitative judgments frozen at recorded times | Can say a prospective registry was created, but not that the judgments are probabilistic, calibrated, or predictive. Human review remains pending. |
| “Forecasting CS breakthroughs” | Exact event predicates cover theorem/algorithm milestones; some are narrower than general breakthrough and events are heterogeneous | Define “breakthrough” operationally as an event satisfying the frozen theorem-level criterion. Avoid general claims about scientific breakthroughs. |
| “Ten questions across CS” | Ten rows with ten encoded subfield labels and nine declared families; purposefully selected | State exact counts and selection limitations; do not infer field coverage or representativeness. |
| “Fourteen leads screened, seven selected, seven excluded” | Narrative counts, identified included IDs and named exclusions; no complete candidate-level audit trail | Attribute to the prior screening record and explicitly state that the flow cannot be independently reconstructed end-to-end. |
| “No outcome yet” | Frozen statuses are not-yet-due, no follow-up outcome IDs, no human adjudication | Supported as current cohort state; not equivalent to negative/no progress. |
| “Missing literature is not failure” | Frozen protocols and explicit missingness categories | Supported as a methodological rule, not a validated empirical result. |
| “Paper 3 targets A and B” | Paper 3 has source-native attention/resolution/premise-revision categories; this project's CS evidence-growth Target B is a separate extension | Say Target A and project-specific Target B are separate; do not relabel Paper 3's source taxonomy or imply B is original Paper 3. |
| “Paper 3 transfers to CS” | No validated CS historical labels or transfer evaluation | Unsupported; state no transfer evaluation. |
| “Paper 4 plans better experiments” | Offline ranking depends on declared assumptions; no matched prospective action outcomes or baseline comparison in this paper | Unsupported; explicitly state no planner utility/superiority result. |
| “Novel evaluation framework” | Related methodology in PreScience and closer same-project astronomy historical/ prospective evaluation | Novelty not established. Describe as adaptation/proposal unless a specific new validated method is demonstrated. |
| “A future AI model can outperform simple baselines” | No labels, model runs, probability forecasts, or performance results | Future research question only; no prediction of superiority. |
| “Cohort is hash-verified” | Manifest checks hashes of cohort, snapshots, and metadata artifacts; source citation hashes are metadata-only | Qualify exactly: registry integrity checks pass; primary source full-text hashes are not comprehensively pinned. |
| “Monitoring is active” | Monitoring is prepared/inactive; zero running crawler, schedule, or worker | Must not claim active tracking. |
| “Two-stage publication plan is established” | Stage 1 may be a future methods/resource contribution; Stage 2 would evaluate matured outcomes | State a research program proposal, not two guaranteed papers. Stage 1 also needs novelty and quality evidence. |
| “The open problem remains open as of issue” | Primary-source search and exact baseline audit; no complete citations/database/version search archive or independent reviewers | Phrase “the cited source states X open; bounded source audit found no matching result” only where documented; never treat search absence as proof of openness. |

## Exact frozen defects and risk disclosures

1. `ORS-POLYNOMIAL-GROWTH-001`: structured deadline 2030-12-31 versus exact event date 2031-12-31. Preserve both; no score/adjudication until resolved from the original preregistration provenance.
2. Original three records: issue/evidence cutoff 2026-10-17 vs nominal 2027-01-01 observation start. Report the early interval separately and preserve original fields.
3. Hashes named as source hashes are hashes of citation metadata or registry artifacts, not source-paper bytes.
4. Human reviewer fields are empty; all source validations in this audit are agent-assisted screening, not independent expert approval.

## Required limitations sentence

“This ten-event, researcher-selected registry is a pilot; it has no matured independently adjudicated outcomes, does not support predictive-performance estimates, and does not establish a novel or validated benchmark. Its source records contain unresolved event-date and source-version limitations.”

The manuscript v0.2 uses this claim ceiling. References in Markdown and TeX must cite the same works and must not elevate internal agent review to peer review.
