## Evidence levels

- `OBSERVED_SOURCE_FACT`: statement directly reported in a dated primary source; author-reported experiments remain attributed, not independently reproduced here.
- `SCIENTIFIC_HYPOTHESIS`: proposed causal or predictive relationship not tested by these records.
- `UNVALIDATED_HEURISTIC`: qualitative ordering rule used for this one portfolio.
- `EMPIRICALLY_SUPPORTED_PREDICTOR`: requires eligible independent outcomes and temporal validation. **No CS feature in this report receives this classification.**

The ledger uses source issue dates and accessible primary pages; it does not archive source full-text bytes. Existing cohort manifests hash declared registry/source-metadata objects, not source PDF contents. Hashes therefore demonstrate record integrity, not the exact text read. The prior technical audit records this limitation explicitly.

## Historical evidence decision

The frozen CS cohorts and follow-up audit artifacts are insufficient for Target A or Target B model evaluation. We did not create a hindsight-selected historical cohort, convert missing evidence into negative labels, or train/score a model. The literature histories used here support qualitative source comparison only. A confirmatory backtest would require a new protocol frozen before later outcomes are inspected; this assignment did not meet that independence condition. The 200-question astronomy prospective instance is relevant prior art and same-project overlap, not independent evidence of CS transfer.
