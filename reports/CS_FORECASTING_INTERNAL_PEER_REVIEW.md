# Internal simulated peer review (not external review)

**Purpose:** stress-test the manuscript from three reviewer perspectives. These are internal, AI-assisted critiques, not independent human peer reviews and not evidence of reviewer agreement.

## Reviewer A — scientific forecasting / evaluation methodology

**Recommendation: reject in current form; invite substantial redesign.** The strongest point is that the paper separates a frozen event, missing evidence, target-specific adjudication, and model performance. But the claimed contribution is not yet demonstrated. PreScience already establishes scientific forecasting as a dataset/benchmark field; the same author has an astronomy historical backtesting protocol and a 200-question prospective cohort. The manuscript must show a specific incremental method, not just a CS port. Ten heterogeneous records with no outcomes cannot establish a useful benchmark or evaluate the protocol. The selection-flow denominator is narratively reported but not reconstructible. Target B is not source-native Paper 3 and its multi-label rubric needs blinded human reliability evidence. The ORS deadline contradiction is fatal for that one event; the old three records have horizon ambiguity. No predictive results are possible.

**Required changes:** remove “benchmark” from title/claim until data-quality/usefulness is demonstrated; formally distinguish same-project predecessor; supply row-level source and screening flow; obtain independent expert event reviews; test adjudication reliability; avoid paper-level model scores until sufficient outcomes mature.

## Reviewer B — algorithms / formal event semantics

**Recommendation: major revision.** Several event records are unusually specific, which is a strength, but exact endpoint matching is not turnkey. Fréchet complexity depends on computational model and exact decision/value reduction. Dynamic matching must fix adversary, probability, update sequence, and explicit output representation. The ORS prefix/suffix formulation can be equivalent by reversing order, but the exact quantifiers and edge-disjointness must be checked. The block-IP event must match variable domains, objective encoding, infeasibility/unboundedness and weak bit complexity. The shallow-circuit multiplicative PTAS must say how gates are encoded and how distance zero is recognized; with a multiplicative guarantee, zero distance requires exact zero output. The privacy event's strict RDP comparison is not fully operational without a fixed comparator, utility measure, and parameter domain. The MAX BISECTION even/odd cardinality and expected-value conventions should be explicit. These are adjudication risks; they do not justify altering the frozen event after observing later outcomes.

**Required changes:** retain each original record; use separate defect/clarification logs; do not score ambiguous cases; have a domain expert sign off on event consistency before any future issuance.

## Reviewer C — benchmark quality, ethics, and publication readiness

**Recommendation: not submission-ready; no evidence supports a two-paper publication guarantee.** Good integrity choices include no numeric probabilities, no search-miss-as-negative, inactive monitoring, and explicit family grouping. Weaknesses are selection bias, tiny pilot size, incomplete candidate-level flow, incomplete source-content version pinning, a coarse family graph, no independent user study, and a same-project adjacent publication. Software checks cannot substitute for scientific validity. The paper also needs clear release/licensing and provenance commitments. Venue fit is conditional: QSS asks for original theoretical/empirical/methodological research on science and requires data essential to claims to be openly shared with a persistent identifier; Research Evaluation requires direct fit with research evaluation theory/practice. NeurIPS 2026 E&D accepted protocols/datasets, but its May 2026 deadline has passed and it requires a meaningful evaluation contribution and accessible documented artifacts. None of these venue scopes implies acceptance.

**Required changes:** do not submit now; secure author approval and disclosure of related same-project manuscripts; make the incremental contribution measurable; resolve data/license and source-hash provenance; provide a public, versioned dataset and a defensible curation methodology; wait for external human review.

All three perspectives converge on the same boundary: methodically careful pilot, but no demonstrated novelty, reliability, or prediction skill. The revised manuscript narrows the title and claim ceiling. The remaining issues require new independent evidence or future data governance; they cannot be solved by stronger prose or more citations.
